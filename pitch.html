1 FeatureCalendar and scheduling

Calendar

“When is my next dentist appointment?”

  • Grete asks by voice; Claudia answers and offers reminders
  • The family shares one calendar and assigns who drives
  • After each visit Claudia asks how it went and tells the family
2 FeaturePill checker

Pill checker

“Have you taken your morning tablets?”

  • The plan arrives by email, WhatsApp or photo. No typing
  • Claudia rings and asks; Grete says taken, not at home or forgot
  • The family is alerted only after 3 strikes, plus low-stock warnings
3 FeatureLive location and emergency alert

Location + help

“Hey Claudia, help!”

  • Jonas sees “At home since 11:20”; normal walks send no push
  • Grete calls for help by voice, even with the phone out of reach
  • Every caregiver gets a full-screen alert with her location
4 FeatureQA and setup

QA + setup

“Shall I tell Jonas?”

  • Setup by voice, with a family code instead of passwords
  • Everyday questions in two calm sentences; no medical advice
  • Grete decides what Jonas hears; nothing private leaves without her yes
