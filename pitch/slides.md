# Claudia · PowerPoint presenter notes

Open the [eleven-slide PowerPoint](Claudia.pptx) in Slide Show mode. During the walkthrough, give the audience a moment to read each new state before advancing.

| Slide | Stage cue |
|---|---|
| **1 · Claudia** | Introduce Grete, who lives on her own, and her children Jonas and Anna. Let the audience hear Claudia’s greeting. |
| **2 · Caregiver strain** | Ask what happens after Jonas hangs up and remembers he meant to ask about Mum’s tablets. Anna may already be making the same call. |
| **3 · Existing products** | Name one recognizable task for each example. DRK Hausnotruf connects an alarm to a responder. MyTherapy reminds people about medication and logs intake. ElliQ offers conversation and a caregiver app. |
| **4 · Claudia’s approach** | Grete speaks. The calendar shows who agreed to help. The diary shows what Grete reported. Invite the audience to follow one family through the screens. |
| **5 · Pair and choose sharing** | Point to the three sharing choices. Grete selects “A few things” before Jonas links his phone with the family code. |
| **6 · Pharmacy** | Grete asks when the pharmacy closes. She chooses a 16:00 visit. Show the saved appointment and the 15:30 reminder. |
| **7 · Shared schedule** | Jonas’s shift changes. Show Anna accepting Friday’s drive, then her name on the dentist appointment. Pickup is at 10:00. The visit is at 10:30. |
| **8 · Medication** | Follow the prescription photo through plan review and reminder setup. At breakfast, Grete confirms she took the tablets. The diary records her answer at 08:04. |
| **9 · Private diary** | Grete mentions a bad night and back pain. Pause on Claudia’s request for permission. After Grete agrees, Jonas sees a short summary. |
| **10 · Location and help** | Grete’s return from the bakery is routine. When she calls for help, her family gets the location. Jonas acknowledges the alert. Grete and Anna can see who is responding. |
| **11 · Closing** | Leave the repository QR code and figures visible. Invite the audience to open the project or try the demo during questions. |

The competitor examples are documented by [DRK Hausnotruf](https://www.drk.de/hilfe-in-deutschland/senioren/altersgerechtes-wohnen/hausnotruf/), [MyTherapy](https://www.mytherapyapp.com/) and [ElliQ](https://elliq.com/pages/faqs).
