# Claudia · Magdehack

Grete asks who is taking her to the dentist. When her son Jonas’s shift changes, her daughter Anna accepts the drive. Friday’s appointment now has a driver, and Grete can hear who is coming. Claudia uses the same voice interface for medication checks, personal notes and calls for help.

## Present the pitch

Open the [eleven-slide PowerPoint](pitch/Claudia.pptx) in Slide Show mode. Slides 1–4 introduce the caregiver challenge, existing products and Claudia’s approach. Slides 5–10 walk through the app one action at a time. Slide 11 closes with the repository QR code and project figures. [Presenter notes](pitch/slides.md)
