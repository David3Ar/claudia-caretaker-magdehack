Features

Not knowing whether Grete is safe is Jonas's constant background worry. Claudia answers "is she all right?" before he has to call, and turns "something is wrong" into one clear action.

  • Hands-free for GreteSenior · every flow

    She says “Hey Claudia” from anywhere in the room. From then on she only talks: no tap to start, no tap to answer, no tap to end. The phone can stay on the table. Buttons are a fallback, never a step.

  • Jonas sees GreteCaregiver · core

    A place name first ("At home since 11:20"), the map second, today's places in order below. Normal movement never sends a push.

  • Grete asks for JonasSenior · open in brackets on the board

    "Where is my son?" gets a coarse answer, "at work", never an address. Each caregiver decides whether Grete may ask.

  • Call JonasSenior → caregiver · direct

    “Hey Claudia, call Jonas” rings him straight away, no question back. Jonas sees “Grete is calling” and answers; Grete is on speaker without touching anything. The Call Jonas button does the same.

  • Call requestSenior → caregiver

    "Ask him to call me" sends one notification with one button: Call Grete. When he calls, Claudia announces it and Grete answers by saying yes.

  • Emergency alertSenior → all caregivers · core

    “Hey Claudia, help!” works when Grete has fallen and can't reach the phone. The coral button and no answer to a check are the other triggers. Every caregiver gets the full-screen alert with where she is.

Flows

Five ways in. Grete never touches the phone in any of them.

  • A · Jonas checks on Grete

    Opens app → status line → pin tab → map and places → Grete gets home, status turns lime quietly.

  • B · “Hey Claudia, where is my son?”

    Wake word → Grete asks → Claudia answers coarse, offers a call → “Yes” → Jonas gets a call request → he calls → Claudia announces it → “Yes” → speaker on.

  • C · “Hey Claudia, help!”

    Grete calls out → coral on her screen and every caregiver's → Jonas taps "I'm on my way" → Claudia tells Grete → Jonas marks her safe.

  • E · “Hey Claudia, call Jonas”

    Wake word and command in one breath → Claudia rings Jonas → he taps Answer → speaker on. If he doesn't pick up, Claudia offers to ask him to call back.

  • D · Grete doesn't answer

    Claudia speaks first, unasked → silence → asked again → amber for Jonas → his call unanswered → coral for everyone. Retries and delays open

Storyboards for each flow are also in 03-live-location.md.
